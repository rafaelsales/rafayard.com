---
title: Why I write here
description: Rafael Sales on why he started this blog — a home for direct, first-person opinions about building and running software.
---
Opinions about software don't age well inside private Slack threads and code review comments. They deserve a permanent address. This blog is that address: my opinions on building software, running it in production, and the trade-offs nobody writes down.

I've spent my career shipping software and forming strong views along the way — about what makes code worth maintaining, what makes teams fast, and which industry habits deserve to die. Most of those views only ever reached the people in the room. Writing them here means they can be found, argued with, and referenced.

Expect text, not visuals. Short when short works, long when the subject earns it. If you want to follow along, [subscribe via RSS](/feed.xml).
